# UK catalogue and artwork

This bounded birthday catalogue prioritises familiar Rossendale garden/upland and Lytham estuary birds. It is not a complete British list. `species.json` is the application seed: stable scientific-name slugs, UK display names, exact search/taxonomic aliases, model labels, and nullable artwork URLs. Missing artwork never invalidates an identification or sighting. Never substitute a similar bird. Unknown model species remain unknown to this catalogue and must retain their raw result.

Current checked result: 183 species, 181 exact photo labels covering 180 of them (the other three are identified by BirdNET audio only), 181 reviewed transparent PNGs (30 upstream plus 151 generated), and 2 explicit missing illustrations (Green Woodpecker and Shag).

Completion batch, 4 November 2026 (evening): every remaining missing bird plus eight local species, same Mac pipeline, prompt recipe, style references, three-attempt cap and fresh-context gpt-6-astra review. The reviewer brief now checks feet by family instead of applying one "three toes forward" rule: woodpeckers, Cuckoo and Ring-necked Parakeet are zygodactyl (two forward, two back); owls may show either arrangement because the outer toe is reversible; Kingfisher is syndactyl; Shag and Gannet are totipalmate; auks, shearwaters and plovers lack a visible hind toe; swimming birds may hide their legs. Under that rule Great Spotted Woodpecker passed (perched, two-forward/two-back feet, white shoulder patches, red nape and undertail). Re-checking accepted birds in those families: Kingfisher and Tawny Owl pass; the upstream Barn and Long-eared Owls keep their forward-facing owl pose and valid feet; the upstream Short-eared Owl lacked the dark eye patches and breast streaking and is replaced by a reviewed generated bird (listed in `REJECTED` in `build_catalogue.py`). Mediterranean Gull (bill too downcurved) and Manx Shearwater (oversized nasal tubes) failed first review and passed on regeneration. Green Woodpecker failed three attempts on the transparency check (a corner pixel at alpha 1) and Shag three on feet not drawn totipalmate; both stay missing. Waxwing uses the upstream illustration.

Local species added in this batch, each only because BirdNET returns it: Lesser Redpoll, Waxwing, Ring Ouzel, Twite, Mandarin Duck, Ring-necked Parakeet (BirdNET "Rose-ringed Parakeet"), Little Gull and Yellow-legged Gull. Five also have an exact photo-model label. Lesser Redpoll, Mandarin Duck and Ring-necked Parakeet are audio-only: the photo model has no Mandarin or parakeet class, and its "Common redpoll" is a different species concept, so it is not mapped. `services/inference/photo-taxonomy.json` must stay identical to `artwork/photo-taxonomy.json` (tested).

GPT-image batch, 4 November 2026: 55 further generated birds, made with the built-in image generation in Codex on the Mac runner. The prompt recipe stayed the same, with four approved birds (Grey Heron, Robin, Oystercatcher, House Sparrow) attached as style references. Joe approved the style from a four-bird sample (Song Thrush, Carrion Crow, Black-headed Gull, Kestrel); slightly warmer colour and denser spotting than the earlier set were accepted. The other 51 were chosen for Rossendale gardens and moorland edge and the Lytham/Fylde estuary. Each passed the generator's own field-mark and Pillow alpha checks. Each then passed a separate fresh-context full-size review (Codex gpt-6-astra) of British-form field marks, anatomy (legs, toes, bill), single left-facing whole bird, transparency and style. The first review failed ten birds, nine for drawing only two forward toes and Bar-tailed Godwit for a hooked bill; all ten were regenerated within three total attempts and passed a second review. Little Grebe, Great Crested Grebe and Wigeon are drawn swimming with legs hidden, which the generation brief allows; the reviewer failed them only for hidden legs, and the lead accepted them on that basis. Per-file notes are in `generated.json`. Swift is drawn in flight. Great Spotted Woodpecker failed three attempts (wrong toe arrangement) and stays missing. Knot and Wheatear were left out of this batch as less likely to be seen. The generated birds are Robin, Blue Tit, Great Tit, Blackbird, Wren, Chaffinch, Oystercatcher, Magpie, Dunnock, Long-tailed Tit, Goldfinch, Woodpigeon, Nuthatch and Grey Heron. All fourteen were individually viewed at full size before admission; detailed feature reviews are recorded per file. Raw native previews of Long-tailed Tit and Grey Heron initially appeared to have halos; inspection showed hidden RGB beneath alpha-zero pixels, and compositing onto cream confirmed clean output. No deterministic pixel edits were used. Missing illustrations include Tree Sparrow, Marsh Tit, Great Spotted Woodpecker and Green Woodpecker; sightings remain supported through the honest unavailable-artwork path.

`uk-species.txt` maintains the chosen names. BirdNET English names come from the checked-in `model/l18n/labels_en.json`; historical genus aliases are explicit. Pied Wagtail maps at species level to Motacilla alba (which also includes White Wagtail); Red Grouse is retained only under the model's Willow Grouse species concept, not represented as an independently identified subspecies. Model output cannot establish subspecies. Blue Tit must never map to African Blue Tit, Robin to American Robin, or European gulls to a newer split without an explicit taxonomy review.

`photo-labels.json` is the 707-class dictionary exported from the real Birder mobilenet_v4_l_eu-common checkpoint (Hugging Face revision a030bb140c795be62c7052053a9f0b915955a39e; checkpoint Apache-2.0). `photo-taxonomy.json` matches exact English labels to curated aliases/BirdNET English names, ignoring only letter case and typographic apostrophes. It does not guess from substrings. Its coverage is deliberately smaller than 707. Python inference should load this map and retain unmapped labels separately.

## Reproduction and verification

From repository root, with Python and Pillow:

```
python artwork/build_catalogue.py
python -m unittest artwork/test_catalogue.py
python app/renderer/build_masks.py
```

The builder copies only the selected, visually approved upstream assets and uses existing generated files with recorded provenance. It never calls an image API. Generated artwork is not bitwise reproducible from a prompt; preserve the checked-in PNGs and their hashes. `assets.json` records each approved file, provenance, licence, modifications, and review scope. `generated.json` records the individual generation sources. `review-upstream.jpg` is the inspected upstream contact sheet, including three rejected candidates. `review-generated.jpg` correctly alpha-composites all fourteen generated cutouts onto cream. Both sheets are review evidence, not application artwork. The admission/test check permits maximum opacity of 254 or 255: the approved Grey Heron output uses 254/255 while its background is fully transparent.
